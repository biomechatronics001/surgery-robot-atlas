About the atlas

A discovery catalogue: each record describes a robot and links to its sources. Listing is descriptive and is not an endorsement.

Who maintains it

The BIRO Lab, New York University. Maintainers: [maintainer names]. Contact: [contact e-mail].

The maintainers built two of the catalogued robots: LegoBot and the portable catheter robot.

How the catalogue was built

  1. Two seed papers: LegoBot (IEEE/ASME T-MECH 2026) and the portable catheter robot (IEEE/ASME T-MECH 2025).
  2. Snowballing: the references of both seeds and the papers citing them and their predecessors gave 393 candidate papers.
  3. Logged searches by robot family, then gap searches for robots they missed, each query recorded with the coverage of the sources it reached (offline arXiv and dblp mirrors, then live bibliographic services), so "not found" is only said after a complete search.
  4. One row per robot line: versions merged, every number with its source key and section, full texts read where available, N.R. where a paper is silent.
  5. Pictures chosen from every paper of a robot's lineage, graded A/B/C, and shown here only when the licence allows.
