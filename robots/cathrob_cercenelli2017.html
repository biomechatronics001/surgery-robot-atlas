CathROB

CathROB remote catheter navigation system (with its 2007-2008 telerobotic predecessor)
University of Bologna, Laboratory of Bioengineering (L. Cercenelli, E. Marcelli, G. Plicchi, B. Bortolani), developed with Tre Esse Progettazione Biomedica · 2007–2017
Family
Academic bedside robots (academic)
Devices
one standard tip-steerable EP catheter; adapters for Navistar Thermocool and EZ-steer bidirectional (Biosense Webster), Sprinklr XL 7 Fr (Medtronic), Blazer II XP (Boston Scientific); tested with Navistar Thermocool 4 mm 7.5 Fr (Sec. 2.1); 2007: RF Enhancr steerable EP catheter (Medtronic) (cercenelli2007 Methods)
Degrees of freedom
3 (advance/withdraw, rotation, tip bend/straighten)
Push
Linear stage
Twist
Handle or knob

Sim-to-real

Testing

Highest level
Animal
What was tested
in vitro silicone heart and vascular mock-up; in vivo 4 sheep (49 +- 4 kg) with right-atrial CARTO mapping and RF ablation at RA isthmus and posterior wall (Sec. 2.2, 3); 2007 predecessor: 3 sheep (58 +- 3 kg), navigation and repositioning to 12 predefined endocardial targets under fluoroscopy, manual vs robotic (cercenelli2007 Methods, Results)
Key numbers
automatic repositioning error 1.5 +- 0.6 mm (left targets) and 1.7 +- 0.5 mm (right; abstract 1.7 +- 0.4); map-to-CT deviation 1.37 +- 0.98 mm; in vivo set-up 5 +- 3 min, maps 63 +- 8 points in 36 +- 7 min with 15 +- 3 min fluoroscopy; speeds 13-18 mm/s, 30-45 deg/s, 2-4 mm/s; no perforation or rhythm change (Sec. 2.1, 3); 2007: navigation 13.0 +- 5.6 s robotic vs 16.1 +- 6.4 s manual, repositioning 9.2 +- 2.5 vs 17.8 +- 7.1 s (both p < 0.001); bench repositioning accuracy 0.2 +- 0.1 mm, 1.4 +- 0.8 mm, 2.1 +- 0.9 mm (longitudinal, rotational, steering) (cercenelli2007 Abstract, Methods)

Mechanism

Push (translation)
linear-stage+clamp (Motion Unit moves the handle held by a spring-preloaded jaw block with silicone-coated rollers; Delrin telescopic sheath to the introducer)
Twist (rotation)
DC micromotor rotates the gripped handle; steering element turned through catheter-specific adapters
Device range
four commercial EP catheter models via adapters
Target anatomy
cardiac electrophysiology mapping and RF ablation (right atrium in sheep; silicone left-atrium model)

Sensing and interface

Force sensing
Honeywell FS-series piezoresistive sensor in the push path; alarm at 60% and stop at 80% of a perforation force of 128 +- 12 gF measured on excised ovine atrium (Sec. 2.1)
Leader and haptics
mock catheter handle plus push-button box; vibration motor and acoustic alarm when thresholds are exceeded; up to four stored targets for automatic repositioning
Imaging
standard fluoroscopy and CARTO electroanatomic mapping; operator outside the radiation field
Sterility
disposable or sterilisable adapters; Motion Unit and arm draped in sterile polyethylene in vivo

Size

Footprint (mm)
Motion Unit 53 x 8 x 11 cm (Fig. 4 labels; text prints 53 x 8 x 110 cm) retracted, 73 x 8 x 11 cm extended (Sec. 2.1)
Mass (kg)
2.3 (Motion Unit); supporting arm 7.5 (Sec. 2.1, Fig. 4)

Lineage
