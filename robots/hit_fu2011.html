HIT master-slave robotic catheter system for a steerable pull-wire catheter
Harbin Institute of Technology, State Key Laboratory of Robotics and System (Yili Fu, Anzhu Gao, Hao Liu, Kai Li, Zhaoguang Liang) with Harbin Medical University · 2011
