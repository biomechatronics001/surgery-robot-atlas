What was tested
R-EVOLUTION 62 pts, 6 sites; registrational trial 145 pts (MicroPort, press); FIH of 2nd-gen 2026 (press)
Key numbers
R-EVOLUTION: technical success 59/62 (95.2%), clinical success 100%, 3 conversions (1 robot-related); robotic 19.9+-9.6 of 39.9+-14.6 min; operator dose -84.5% under apron (durand2023_evaluation Results, partial). 15 systems installed worldwide (medtechdive, web).
