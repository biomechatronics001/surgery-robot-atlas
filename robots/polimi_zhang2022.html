MitraClip stabiliser

PoliMi motorised stabiliser for the MitraClip delivery catheter with IRL path planning
Politecnico di Milano, DEIB NEARLab (E. De Momi; X. Zhang, A. Segato) with E. Votta (cardiovascular biomechanics); Horizon 2020 funding · 2022
Family
Academic bedside robots (academic)
Devices
Abbott MitraClip delivery catheter, 16 Fr (5.28 mm), 109.5 cm, three pull wires 90 deg apart, inside its sheath (Sec. III-C)
Degrees of freedom
3 (medio-lateral bending, antero-posterior bending, insertion in the sheath)
Push
Linear stage
Twist
None
Tested up to
Phantom
Sim-to-real
Yes — A controller or perception model trained in simulation ran on this robot.

Testing

Highest level
Phantom
What was tested
patient-specific silicone phantom of femoral vein and IVC with rigid fossa ovalis and mitral replicas registered to Unity (Sec. IV-A)
Key numbers
closed-loop tip error 1.12 +- 0.75, 1.09 +- 0.68, 1.66 +- 0.62 mm (X/Y/Z), maximum 2.64 mm; PID reduces mean error by 32.68% vs open loop; planned path 60.63 +- 0.96 mm (BC+GAIL) vs 64.99 +- 6.11 mm (manual) (Sec. V)

Mechanism

Push (translation)
linear-stage+clamp (linear actuator on the motorised stabiliser advances the delivery catheter)
Twist (rotation)
none; two bending DOF actuated through the handle
Device range
one clinical delivery system
Target anatomy
structural heart: transseptal mitral edge-to-edge repair

Sensing and interface

Force sensing
none
Leader and haptics
none (autonomous execution of planned path, PID on EM feedback)
Imaging
NDI Aurora EM: 5-DOF tip sensor (0.7 +- 1.4 mm) for feedback, 6-DOF probe (0.48 +- 0.88 mm) for recording
Sterility
N.R.

Size

Footprint (mm)
N.R.
Mass (kg)
N.R. (3-D-printed PLA)

Simulation

Simulation model
yes: Unity scene of the patient anatomy with a Cosserat-rod-based catheter kinematic model (zhang2022_robotic Sec. III)
Sim-trained controller
yes (phantom): a BC+GAIL inverse-RL path planner trained in the Unity environment generated the path that the robot executed in the physical phantom with EM closed loop (zhang2022_robotic Sec. IV, Fig. 7)
