ROSES

ROSES (RObotic System for Endovascular Surgery)
Calabrian High Tech (CHT S.r.l.; G. Danieli) with Magna Graecia University (C. Indolfi, A. Polimeni, S. De Rosa) · 2018–2023
Family
Commercial and clinical-stage systems (clinical-stage)
Devices
guidewire plus balloon or stent catheter per slave; first-in-human: 6 Fr JR4 guide, 0.014 in BMW wire, 2.0 x 12 mm balloon, 2.5 x 15 mm DES (indolfi2023_first)
Degrees of freedom
3 per slave (catheter advance/retract, guidewire advance/retract, common rotation); one console drives up to three slaves (techbriefs 2019 entry)
Push
Friction rollers
Twist
Rotating drive unit
Tested up to
Human
Sim-to-real
None found — No simulation-trained controller found for this robot.

Testing

Highest level
Human
What was tested
in vitro tubing and X-ray tests; first patient 22 Jan 2019 (two stents), trials 'still in progress' in 2022 (danieli2022_development Sec. 2); 2023 case report of one 64-year-old patient (R-PCI of a PL branch)
Key numbers
case: procedure 35 min, contrast 110 mL, stent result satisfactory at 2.5-year follow-up (indolfi2023_first); fluoroscopy time N.R.

Mechanism

Push (translation)
friction-roller (3-D-printed disposables convert gear rotation into advance/retract through friction wheels)
Twist (rotation)
the disposable is rotated by a concentric gear train in the robot actuator (danieli2022_development Sec. 2)
Device range
0.014 in guidewires to large catheters (36 mm passage in v3); exact Fr range N.R.
Target anatomy
coronary PCI; endovascular surgery, large catheters for aneurysm repair or TAVI (cart version)

Sensing and interface

Force sensing
resistance to advancement shown on the console; cart version measures forces opposed by the patient with a force transducer (danieli2022_development abstract, Sec. 3)
Leader and haptics
passive master console with joysticks per track (one for the guidewire, one for devices); no force rendering reported
Imaging
fluoroscopy; cockpit inside or outside the cath lab
Sterility
sterile disposables in contact with the devices, split upper/lower so they can be removed without withdrawing the device (danieli2022_development abstract)

Lineage
2018 PCT patent (ref. 6 of indolfi2023_first); robot actuator v1 (motor axes parallel to the gear train), v2 (motors perpendicular, thinner), v3 (36 mm central passage for haemostasis valve and large catheters) (danieli2022_development Fig. 12); disposables for angioplasty (DA), big catheters (DEBC), movable-core guidewires and a tip-curvature catheter; cart with rail and force transducer for endovascular surgery (Fig. 13); first patient 22 Jan 2019; 2023 case report
