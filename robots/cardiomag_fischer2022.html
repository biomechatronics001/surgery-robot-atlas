CardioMag

CardioMag eMNS with magnetic catheter and magnetic localisation (ETH)
Nelson, ETH Zurich (Fischer, Boehler) · 2022
Family
Magnetic navigation (magnetic-navigation)
Devices
own catheter: OD 2.8 mm / ID 2 mm Flexan tube with 3 mm OD x 6 mm permanent magnet at tip, Hall sensor 12 mm behind magnet
Degrees of freedom
field direction and gradient from 8 electromagnets; advance by Maxon-motor catheter advancer at 10 mm/s

Testing

Highest level
Phantom
What was tested
2D vascular models a and b; catheter navigated through all branches; localisation tested over 40 trials on path 1-3 of model a
Key numbers
eMNS fields 20 mT, gradient 0.15 mT/mm; mean localisation error 3.64 mm (sd 1.61) model b, 2.74 mm with extra field change; model a mean 7.94 mm (sd 2.94), 5.65 mm with extra gradient; position estimate 38.3 ms
