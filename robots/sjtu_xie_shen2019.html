SJTU hybrid drive

SJTU master-slave vascular intervention robot with progressive (friction + clamp) drive and force display
Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Biomedical Engineering (Le Xie; Hao Shen, Cheng Wang, Shuang Wang, Zheng Liu) with Chinese Academy of Sciences and Peking Union Medical College Hospital · 2019–2024

Testing

Highest level
Animal
What was tested
shen2019: bench performance tests plus four in vivo swine trials including carotid stent revascularisation; wang2024: silicone left-coronary model, five repeats per condition
Key numbers
Translational error <= 0.45 %, rotational error <= 2.6 deg, contrast injection error < 0.2 mL (shen2019 abstract); safety threshold 0.3 N and distal error larger than proximal error (wang2024, S8 evidence)

Mechanism

Push (translation)
friction-roller + clamp hybrid ('progressive' drive; guidewire clamps on a shifting board, balloon catheter on a friction module; shen2019 Fig. 1c)
Twist (rotation)
Guidewire rotation by the actuator (mechanism detail not extracted; text of shen2019 is column-mangled)
Device range
0.035 in guidewire (wang2024)
Target anatomy
carotid and coronary (swine; left coronary silicone model)

Sensing and interface

Force sensing
Force and torque at the drive (shen2019); wang2024 adds force sensors at the proximal guidewire and electromagnetic (Aurora) sensors at both ends for delivered motion
Leader and haptics
Omega 3 (Force Dimension) in a console with display (shen2019 Fig. 1a); MRF leader in liu2022
Imaging
X-ray angiography in the swine trials; MR N.R.

Lineage
xiang2019_masterslave (RO-MAN 2019: guidewire between two electric grippers on a lead-screw base, balloon catheter by friction wheels, two master manipulators, Kalman filter) -> shen2019_novel (IJCARS 2019: hybrid progressive drive, contrast injector, Omega 3 leader, in vivo swine) -> liu2022_magnetorheological (MRF leader, abstract not read) -> wang2024_improved (force sensors integrated, admittance control); 2016-17: earlier SJTU (Le Xie, Hao Shen) lead-screw catheter/guidewire pusher with a chuck on an ATI Nano43 six-axis sensor (Lin et al. 2016, Machine Design & Research), run by Wuhan Univ. of Technology as MISR with a Phantom Omni, 3:1 motion scaling and vibration warning, PVC-tube phantom (Xie 2017 ACIRS; Alekseeva 2025 Table 3 row 3, Fig. 10b) (xie2017_masterslave; alekseeva2025_design); 2012: guidewire tele-manipulation system with a laser-mouse-sensor motion detector as leader and a guidewire manipulation appliance, tested by operating time against tip-to-target distance and angle (Shen 2012 Zhongguo Yi Liao Qi Xie Za Zhi 36:32-35, PMID 22571149); group link only by Zhao 2022 Table 1 row 10c (PubMed affiliation: The 455 Hospital of PLA, Shanghai 200240) (shen2012_design; zhao2022_remote)
