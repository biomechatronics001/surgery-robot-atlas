CathPilot

CathPilot (expandable cable-driven tip platform steered through a 3-D cam handle)
Toronto Metropolitan University (M. A. Tavallaei) with Sunnybrook Research Institute (G. A. Wright), University of Toronto (A. Dueck), Magellan Biomedical, University of Waterloo · 2022–2023
Family
Actuated-tip devices (actuated-device)
Devices
CathPilot catheter positions a standard guidewire tip; frame deliverable through a 12 Fr sheath (OD 5.3 mm), expanding to 20 mm (TMECH 2022 Sec. III)
Degrees of freedom
2 (planar positioning of the guidewire end-effector within the expanded frame)
Push
Tip-actuated

Testing

Highest level
Animal
What was tested
TBME 2023: tortuous vessel phantom (target reach vs non-steerable and steerable catheters), ex-vivo human CTO samples (fresh and fixed), in-vivo porcine aorta in one male pig of 56.6 kg
Key numbers
target-reach success 31% non-steerable, 69% steerable, 100% CathPilot; CTO crossing 83% fresh and 100% fixed; up to four times higher force delivery and pushability (TBME 2023 abstract); submillimetre positioning (TMECH 2022 abstract)

Mechanism

Push (translation)
tendon/tip-actuation (four PTFE cables, 0.012 in, from handle to an end-effector suspended in an expandable frame)
Twist (rotation)
N.R. (planar 2-DOF)
Device range
standard guidewire through the end-effector; several frame opening diameters tested
Target anatomy
peripheral arteries, chronic total occlusion (CTO) crossing

Sensing and interface

Force sensing
none (passive)
Leader and haptics
handle with a 2-DOF 3-D cam surface sets all four cable lengths: fully manual mechanical teleoperation, no motors (TMECH 2022 Sec. III)
Imaging
