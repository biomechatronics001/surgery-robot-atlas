Robotic microcatheter

Self-steering robotic microcatheter (series-elastic, two bending segments)
Simaan, Vanderbilt (ARMA lab) with Chitale, VUMC · 2024
Family
Actuated-tip devices (actuated-device)
Devices
own notched-nitinol catheter: outer tube OD 1.2 mm / ID 0.95 mm, intermediate 0.89/0.725, inner 0.66 OD with 0.475 mm bore (S8 check of abah2024 Sec. IV-A); roughly 3.6 Fr at maximum OD (1.2 mm / 0.333, my conversion)
Degrees of freedom
4 (insertion, roll, proximal bend, distal bend; bending in two separate planes)
Push
Tip-actuated
Twist
Rotating drive unit
Tested up to
Phantom
Sim-to-real
Model-based only — Commands computed in a simulator or from a physical model ran on the robot; nothing was learned.

Testing

Highest level
Phantom
What was tested
2D vasculature model: branch selection left and right, insertion into unknown channel under active compliance, one autonomous deployment along 2D ICA path; no success counts
Key numbers
distal segment OD about 0.88-1.2 mm (text differs, see S8 note); compliance trigger 0.01 N; autonomous insertion errors larger than simulated because passive extension floats between actuation unit and phantom entry

Mechanism

Push (translation)
tendon/tip-actuation
Twist (rotation)
custom actuation unit roll DoF
Device range
N.R.
Target anatomy
neuroendovascular (ICA path, 2D vasculature models)

Sensing and interface

Force sensing
joint-level load sensing via series-elastic actuation; active compliance triggers at 0.01 N tip force (about 1 g)
Leader and haptics
no
Imaging
bi-plane fluoroscopy for tracking (Fig. 11); catheter tracking and pose filtering algorithms
Sterility
N.R.

Simulation

Simulation model
kinematic model with calibration and simulated path following along 2D ICA (Fig. 8) (abah2024 Sec. V, full)
Sim-trained controller
model-based: planner configurations computed in simulation were run on the hardware; hardware position errors exceeded simulated errors (abah2024 Sec. V-C, full); no learned policy
