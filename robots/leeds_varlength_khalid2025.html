Leeds variable-length magnetically steer

Leeds variable-length magnetically steered catheter tip: 3D-printed soft helical tube with a tip magnet and a nitinol tendon (hybrid tendon and magnetic actuation)
STORM Lab, School of Electronic and Electrical Engineering, University of Leeds (M. A. Khalid, P. Valdastri, J. H. Chandler) (khalid2025_variablelength p. 1) · 2025
Family
Magnetic navigation (magnetic-navigation)
Devices
own catheter tip only: SLA-printed elastic-resin helical tube (Elastic 50A-V2), 2.4 mm OD, 0.8 mm ID, 18 mm nominal length (2 mm magnet housing, 16 mm helix, 5 mm base), 1.58 x 1.58 mm axially magnetised N42 magnet, 0.004 in (101.6 um) nitinol wire through the centre; proximal catheter shaft with lumen not yet fitted (Sec. II-B, V)
Degrees of freedom
2: tip length (tendon) and tip bending by field direction; bending tested in one plane only ('only performed in a 2D plane'); catheter base fixed in all tests (Sec. II-C3, II-C4, III-D)
Push
Magnetic
Twist
Field direction
Tested up to

Testing

Highest level
Phantom
What was tested
bench: tendon actuation without field in 1 mm (100-step) increments (Fig. 4); magnetic actuation at three lengths with the field angle swept in 30 deg steps to +-175 deg at 20 mT (Fig. 5), compared with the model (Fig. 6); phantom: workspace of the variable-length tip against a fixed-length tip in the 26.3 mm ICA sac (lower 57.5 % reachable without moving the base) and in the overlapping 12 and 20 mm sacs (Sec. III-D, Fig. 7, supplementary video); run counts N.R.
Key numbers
max tip angle 68.6 / 81.5 / 89.5 deg (contracted 13 mm / normal 18 mm / extended 23.6 mm) at 20 mT (Sec. III-C); length change -6.7 mm (-37.2 %) and +6.4 mm (+35.6 %) for tendon input -7 and +8 mm, near 1:1 in contraction, transmission loss by tendon buckling in extension (Sec. III-B); model RMSE of tip deflection 0.42 / 1.1 / 2.01 mm with a fixed stiffness (75 GPa), 0.64 / 0.96 / 1.78 mm after refitting the stiffness per length (Young's modulus 107.97 / 129.21 / 152.98 GPa); tip-angle RMSE 29.29 / 31.82 / 36.53 deg before and 16.35 / 15.25 / 17.8 deg after refit (Sec. III-C)

Mechanism

Push (translation)
magnetic (steering of the tip by the field direction of an MFG-100 electromagnetic system, 20 mT, Sec. II-C1, II-C3); tip length by a nitinol tendon pushed and pulled by a stepper linear actuator (Actuonix S20-100-38-B, 0.01 mm step, Arduino): tendon/tip-actuation, -37.2 % to +35.6 % (Sec. II-C1, II-C2, III-B); no advance mechanism: the base is fixed
Twist (rotation)
magnetic torque of the external field on the tip magnet; no axial rotation (Sec. II-A, II-C3)
Device range
tip OD 2.4 mm; length 11.3 mm (fully contracted) to 24.4 mm (fully extended), 18 mm normal (Sec. III-B, IV)
Target anatomy
endovascular treatment of carotid artery aneurysm ('carotid artery aneurysm intervention', Abstract; coil embolization, Sec. I, Fig. 1); tested in two 3D-printed 2D aneurysm phantoms: a 26.3 mm internal carotid artery sac and an overlapping 12 mm and 20 mm complex sac (Sec. II-C4, III-D, Fig. 7)

Leader and haptics

Imaging
