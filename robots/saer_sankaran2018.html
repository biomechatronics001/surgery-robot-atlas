SAER Surgeon Augmented Endovascular Robotic system

Testing

Highest level
Phantom
What was tested
simple and complex branching phantoms; count of runs N.R.; 2020: load-cell bench with a short phantom section and guidewire navigation in a simple branching phantom with a haptic alert at the block (sankaran2020 Figs. 2, 9)
Key numbers
stop/haptic threshold 1.5 N; force estimate validated at 30 and 50 rpm (curves, no error number); 2020: guidewire feed 15.7 mm/s at 30 rpm; bias force 0.6716 N at 30 rpm; unbiased estimate vs load cell RMS error 0.22 N (30 rpm) and 0.23 N (50 rpm) (sankaran2020 Results, Fig. 8)

Mechanism

friction-roller (knurled rollers)
Twist (rotation)
motor rotating the roller box about the tool axis (slip ring)

N.R.
Target anatomy
branching vascular phantoms (one mimics bovine aortic arch)

Sensing and interface

none; reaction force estimated from motor current, calibrated by bilevel optimisation, checked against a load cell
Leader and haptics
sensing unit with encoder-read rollers on a real tool; vibratory/audio alert above 1.5 N (no force reflection)
