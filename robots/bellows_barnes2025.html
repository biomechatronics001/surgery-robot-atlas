Bellows guidewire

Soft fluidic bellows guidewire with teleoperated drive and imitation-learning policy
Krieger lab, Johns Hopkins, with Sochol (University of Maryland) · 2025

What was tested
36 training mazes from 1584 possible, rearranged test set 3 mazes, novel test set 3 mazes; 647 demonstrations (218 normal, 429 recovery incl. DAgger)
Key numbers
83 % success on unseen layouts, 75 % on unseen patient-derived geometry (abstract); ablations 22 % to 61 % success; segmentation tip error 0.6 mm median, 1.9 mm 95th, 8.0 mm max
