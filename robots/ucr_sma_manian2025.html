UC Riverside soft bidirectional SMA actu

UC Riverside soft bidirectional SMA actuator catheter: silicone body with a U-shaped SMA wire and a pre-stretched elastic band on a braid-reinforced shaft
University of California Riverside, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering (J. Sheng, K. Oda, P. AfshariNejad, K. Lee, A. Akbari) and Dept. of Materials Science and Engineering (V. Manian) (manian2025_soft p. 1 footnote) · 2025
Family
Actuated-tip devices (actuated-device)
Devices
own steerable catheter robot: soft SMA actuator (Dragon Skin 10 silicone, U-shaped SMA wire 0.5 mm x 130 mm, elastic band 16 mm stretched to 36 mm) joined by heat shrink to a braid-reinforced tube shaft (Duke Extrusion); one device (Sec. II-A, VI)
Degrees of freedom
1 active bending DOF (bidirectional, one plane); insertion and rotation by hand at the base (Sec. VI, Fig. 1)
Push
Tip-actuated

Testing

Highest level
Phantom
What was tested
bench: quasi-static bending angle vs SMA temperature, 3 cycles (Sec. V-D, Fig. 6); 150 heating-cooling cycles, 2.4 A for 11 s, 35 s cooling (Sec. V-B, Fig. 5b); surface temperature in 37 C water (Sec. V-C); phantom: three demonstrations, one per artery, each within 30 s (Sec. VI, Fig. 7); no repeat counts or success rates
Key numbers
bending -70 to +71 deg, model R2 0.995, RMSE 4.3 deg (martensite to austenite) and 2.0 deg (reverse) (Sec. V-D; Fig. 6 caption prints 4.2 deg); max difference between the three cycles 3.7 and 3.1 deg; range falls over the first 60 cycles, to -59.7 / +59.4 deg at 100 cycles (Sec. V-B); highest surface temperature about 46 C at SMA 70 C (Sec. V-C); demo bends of about -36 deg in 2 s (SMA at 51 C) and about -47 deg in 2.2 s (54 C) (Sec. VI)

Mechanism

Push (translation)
tendon/tip-actuation: Joule heating of the SMA wire under micro-temperature-sensor feedback bends the tip between positive and negative curvature (Sec. II-A, V-A); insertion by hand ('translating and rotating the base of the robot', Sec. VI)
Twist (rotation)
by hand at the base of the shaft (Sec. VI)
Device range
actuator 'sub-5 mm envelope', diagonal size to be cut to 3 mm in future work (Sec. VII); shaft diameter and length N.R.; Table I geometry not in the text layer
Target anatomy
cardiovascular catheterization, 'diagnostic evaluation and therapeutic interventions' with stent delivery and angioplasty named (Sec. I); demonstrated in a 3D-printed half-open type 1 aortic arch phantom: left subclavian, left common carotid and brachiocephalic arteries (Sec. VI, Fig. 7)

Sensing and interface

Force sensing
none (micro temperature sensors on the actuator; load cell only for the elastic-band characterisation, Sec. IV-A)
Leader and haptics
none; the user sets the SMA temperature by hand and watches the phantom directly (Sec. VI)
Imaging
optical: direct visual feedback of the phantom (Sec. VI); fluoroscopy and MRI N.R.
Sterility
N.R.; the Discussion plans for single-use surgical robots with finite motion cycles, likely < 100 (Sec. VII)
