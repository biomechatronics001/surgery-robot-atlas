RACS braided-sleeve-gripper catheterisation system
KU Leuven (Vander Poorten, Ourak) with FBGS International · 2023
Family
Academic bedside robots (academic)
Devices
generic catheters and guidewires, one at a time (8 Fr ablation catheter and 8 Fr sheath used)

Sim-to-real
None found — No simulation-trained controller found for this robot.

Testing

Highest level
Animal
What was tested
bench force-control comparison (3 controllers, 3 runs each); feasibility in one live 40 kg swine (femoral incision)
Key numbers
design targets: 40 mm/s, grip force >= 4.5 N, grip torque >= 15 N.mm; contact-force error with best controller 0.0020+-0.0119 N (12.7% of 0.2 N setpoint); the 2023 stroke is unlimited

Mechanism

Push (translation)
clamp-relay (pair of pneumatic braided-sleeve grippers on two linear DC servomotors, dynamic regripping, infinite stroke)
Twist (rotation)
spur-gear ring turning the whole frame about the instrument axis (maxon EC-i 40)
Device range
N.R. (design target: generic instruments)
Target anatomy
cardiac ablation (beating-heart contact force); femoral-to-heart route in swine

Sensing and interface

Force sensing
multi-core FBG fibre in the catheter (pose and tip contact force); Hall sensors on the drive
Leader and haptics
none; joystick
Imaging
monoplane fluoroscopy; MRI N.R.
Sterility
N.R.

Versions

Lineage
2023 (single); 2015: KU Leuven teleoperated active catheter for TAVI: 2-DoF catheter driver (two DC motors: roller insertion, hollow-shaft rotation) plus pneumatic-artificial-muscle distal bending, 4-DoF haptic leader, three joystick mappings compared in an artificial aorta model (Rosa 2015 IROS) (Belikov 2024 Fig. 6 RO; Zhao 2022 Table 1 row 22) (rosa2015_intuitive; belikov2024_evolution; zhao2022_remote)
