Model source and kinematics

51-legobot-ros2/description/urdf/legobot.urdf and description/meshes/ (the ROS 2 / RViz digital twin with the CAD housings; generated from the CAD by 70-legobot-simulation/scripts/build_urdf_modules.py). Joint values are tube outputs, not motor angles (70-legobot-simulation/KINEMATICS.md): per module rot = 0.26667 x motor rot, ins = 0.032 m/rad x (motor ins - motor rot). Devices are nested: the guide catheter's insertion is upper_ins, the inner catheter's upper_ins + middle_ins, the guidewire's upper_ins + middle_ins + lower_ins. The URDF's joint limits are placeholders; the slider ranges are display ranges. Lab and collaborator material (BIRO Lab, New York University); CAD not released — internal build only.

Testing

Highest level
Phantom
What was tested
benchtop: 8 Fr catheter slip test; buckling-load test with 5 devices; silicone neurovascular phantom (Type III arch): angiography with 2 modules and aspiration thrombectomy with 3 modules (clot aspirated); one demonstration each, repeat counts N.R.; one timed module exchange 1 min 50 s
Key numbers
peak insertion force 5.3 N and torque 8.4 mN.m (8 Fr) vs requirement 4.5 N / 8 mN.m; buckling load 0.1 N (0.014 in wire) to 4.3 N (8 Fr); coupler: 5 full microcatheter rotations vs ~180 deg without; 500 Hz command loop; 345x249x115 mm for 3 instruments vs 360x250x250 mm (2 instruments) of Kantu per Table I

Mechanism

Push (translation)
friction-roller
Twist (rotation)
clamped-wheel rotation: hollow-shaft direct-drive motor (0.9 N.m peak) turns the clamped instrument; module coupler adds a second torque input via bevel gears; decoupled from translation by command compensation
Device range
0.33-3 mm (adjustable clamp)
Target anatomy
neurovascular (diagnostic angiography, aspiration thrombectomy); coronary and peripheral stated as applicable

Sensing and interface

Force sensing
motor side only (current, dual 17-bit encoders); no direct instrument sensing (stated limitation)
Leader and haptics
none; Xbox gamepad teleoperation; haptic feedback not yet implemented
Imaging
N.R. (not tested)
Sterility
not validated (stated future work); preloaded replaceable modules; standard Luer lock / Y-connector interfaces
