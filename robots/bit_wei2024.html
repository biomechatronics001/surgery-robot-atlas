Magnetic navigation · bit_wei2024

Magnetic wire robot

Magnetically actuated guidewire robot (0.9 mm PDMS and NdFeB tip on a guidewire core), steered by a permanent magnet and characterised in three-axis Helmholtz coils
Beijing Institute of Technology, School of Automation (Siyi Wei, Jinhui Zhang, Zhiwei Wu) and School of Medical Technology (Duanduan Chen) (wei2024_magnetic p. 12592) · 2024
Family
Magnetic navigation (magnetic-navigation)
Devices
its own magnetic guidewire robot: PDMS with NdFeB particles (6.5 um, 66.7 wt%) moulded around a stiff core, the 0.47 mm x 3 m V-18 ControlWire (Boston Scientific), with a 0.2 mm silicone shell and a hydrophilic PVP coating; outer diameter 900 um (Sec. II-III)
Degrees of freedom
tip deflection in any direction set by the outside field (three field axes in the coil tests); advance and retraction by the operator's hand (Sec. IV-A, V)

Testing

Highest level
Phantom
What was tested
phantom: single and double circular navigation in a 2-D branch phantom (inner diameter 6 mm, bifurcations of 98 and 120 deg) with an NdFeB magnet in repulsive or attractive mode while the operator pushes the wire (Sec. IV-A, Fig. 2, movies M1-M2); selective navigation into the right external and internal carotid branches of a 3-D cardio-cerebrovascular silicone phantom with pulsatile flow of a blood analogue, 40 % glycerin (Sec. IV-B, Figs. 3-4, movies M3-M4); bench: deflection against field strength in three orthogonal pairs of Helmholtz coils, x and y coils 0-22 A in 2 A steps, z coil 0-2.2 A in 0.2 A steps (Sec. V, Figs. 5-6). Number of runs N.R.
Key numbers
deflection up to about 120 deg with the magnet (Sec. II, IV); in the coils 20 and 26 deg (counterclockwise, clockwise) at 8.58 mT in x, 21 and 31 deg at 9.17 mT in y, vertical displacement 11.05 and 13.55 mm at 5.87 mT in z (Sec. V); deflection against field strength R2 0.994-0.999 (Sec. V-C); magnetisation 95 kA/m, Young's modulus 1.68 MPa (Sec. III); magnet N52, 50 mm diameter x 100 mm (Sec. II); uniform-field region 36 x 36 x 36 mm at 1 % (Sec. VI)

Mechanism

Push (translation)
magnetic (steering); forward push by hand ('the translation of the magnetically actuated robot is controlled by the push/pull forces under the manipulation of the operator', Sec. IV-A)
Twist (rotation)
tip direction by the outside field: repulsive or attractive steering with an NdFeB magnet (flipped to switch the mode), or the uniform field of three orthogonal pairs of Helmholtz coils; no shaft rotation (Sec. II, IV, V)
Device range
outer diameter 900 um; core wire 0.47 mm x 3 m; robot-to-vessel diameter ratio 0.2-0.24 : 1 (Sec. II-III)
Target anatomy
coronary intervention (PCI) as the stated motivation (Sec. I); sized for the carotid ('external carotid artery with typical diameters of about 5.2 +/- 1.0 mm', Sec. II); navigated in a 2-D branch phantom (inner diameter 6 mm) and into the right external and internal carotid branches of a 3-D cardio-cerebrovascular silicone phantom (Sec. IV, Figs. 2-4)
