Hydraulic catheter

Hydraulic steerable microcatheter tip (hyperelastic, saline-driven chambers)
Friend lab, UC San Diego (Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering) with UCSD Neurosurgery, Radiology and Surgery; concept co-devised with Bernard Yan, Melbourne Brain Centre (gopesh2021_soft p. 1, 12) · 2021
Family
Actuated-tip devices (actuated-device)
Devices
own hydraulic microcatheter: 900 um outer diameter with four 50 um saline channels and a 400 um lumen, 15 mm steerable tip, 160 cm long Pebax shaft (gopesh2021_soft p. 3-5, 9); used through a 5 F guide with a 0.014-in Synchro2 micro-guidewire and AXIUM PRIME coils (p. 10)
Degrees of freedom
3D tip orientation by pressurising one or more of four channels; no DOF count (gopesh2021_soft p. 1, 3)
Push
Tip-actuated
Twist
None
Tested up to
Animal
Sim-to-real
None found — No simulation-trained controller found for this robot.

Testing

Highest level
Animal
What was tested
2 Yucatan miniature pigs (47 kg), one per acute terminal session (gopesh2021_soft p. 11): navigation external carotid -> ascending pharyngeal -> parotid artery and coil deployment; patient-derived silicone phantoms (femoral to ICA; ICA to a PCOM aneurysm) with pulsatile flow, trial count N.R.
Key numbers
180-degree bend at 350 kPa, rupture at 1200 kPa (safety factor about 4); response time < 1 s through 1.6 m of 50 um tubing; length-to-diameter ratio 10,000:1; no endothelial injury or perforation on angiography (gopesh2021_soft p. 3-10)

Mechanism

Push (translation)
tendon/tip-actuation (hydraulic: four saline channels bend the tip; insertion by hand)
Twist (rotation)
none reported: bending direction set by which channel is pressurised; shaft rotation N.R.
Device range
160 cm; 180-degree bend (curvature 400 1/m) at 350 kPa, rupture at 1200 kPa (gopesh2021_soft p. 4)
Target anatomy
neuro: in vivo external carotid -> ascending pharyngeal -> parotid artery with coil deployment in the parotid artery (not intracranial); PCOM aneurysm coiling in a silicone phantom (gopesh2021_soft p. 6-9)

Sensing and interface

Force sensing
none
Leader and haptics
no: handheld controller connected by microtubing (p. 9); in vivo a 1-ml syringe per channel locked with a one-way stopcock (p. 6); bench: Elveflow OB-1 pressure controller (p. 9)
Imaging
fluoroscopic roadmap (Siemens Artis Zeego); gold radiopaque markers at three positions and barium-filled Pebax (gopesh2021_soft p. 5, 10)
Sterility
N.R.

Simulation

Simulation model
ANSYS finite-element model of the tip (two-parameter Mooney-Rivlin) to choose channel radius and radial position; the channel count was fixed at four by the authors (gopesh2021_soft p. 4, 10)
