LIBERTY

LIBERTY Endovascular Robotic System
Microbot Medical · 2024–2026
Family
Commercial and clinical-stage systems (commercial)
Devices
guide catheter, microcatheter, guidewire (telescopic); standard 0.014-0.018-in guidewires with compatible microcatheters (2-3 F) and guide catheters (4-6 F) (cornelis2026_in p. 3); in the 20-patient study the robot advanced microwire and microcatheter (0.014-0.016-in wires, 2.7 F Progreat or Truselect), guide catheters (typically 5 F) placed by hand (cornelis2026_in p. 3-5, Fig. 2b); tested 0.016-in microwire, 2.4 F microcatheter, 5 F guide (vidal2024_performance)
Degrees of freedom
3 (gao2026_legobot via S8); advance, retract, rotate (vidal2024_performance)

Testing

Highest level
Human
What was tested
20 pts treated (21 screened), 3 centres (MSKCC, Miami Cardiac & Vascular Institute, Brigham and Women's), 3 interventional radiologists, 3-day follow-up; Y-90 mapping 10, UFE 3, haemangioma 2, TACE 2, sacral, intercostal and pulmonary 1 each; robot removed before embolisation (cornelis2026_in); 2 pigs x 6 operators (vidal2024_performance); simulator benchmark
Key numbers
navigation to target without conversion 20/20 (95% CI 83.2-100%); robotic navigation 6.3+-7.6 min (median 3, range 1-32) of 120.1+-34.5 min procedure; fluoroscopy median 7.9 min; operator dose median 1.4 uSv (abstract; 1.46 uSv in Table 2) (cornelis2026_in). Wiring on PCI simulator 27.1 s vs manual 36.9 s vs GRX 62.3 s (winsor2024_performance). FDA 510(k) K243789 decided 2025-09-04.

Sterility
